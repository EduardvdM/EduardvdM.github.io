Fieldwork in Maua, Kenya

Study Design Vignette Cards Pebble Voting Game Gallery Where & When

How do religious leaders, scripture, and community shape our beliefs and social norms?

In 2026, I designed and led an original survey in Maua, Meru County, Kenya, for my job market paper, Preaching Against the Choir: Religious Leaders and Reproductive Norms in Kenya, in partnership with Sodzo Kenya.

Paper Data
  • 635women surveyed
  • 17religious leaders
  • 4focus groups
  • 20enumerators trained
  • Kimerusurvey language
Key Finding

Religious leaders are most effective when preaching against an entrenched social norm.

Read the Abstract

Study Design

↑

Each woman heard Kendi's story, told on four picture cards: who Kendi is, her community, her religious leader, and her reflection on scripture. The community norm, the leader's stance, and the scripture passage were varied at random, so comparing answers shows how much each one shapes views on family planning.
