I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Rice University{% if site.job_market %} and on the 2026–2027 job market{% else %} in Houston, Texas{% endif %}. My research in applied microeconomics centers on poverty, culture, religion, and social norms, with a primary focus on the African continent.

My work examines individual pathways out of poverty and the roots of inter-group inequality. It underscores the pitfalls of one-size-fits-all policies in culturally diverse settings and explores how religion and religious leaders shape and interact with deep-rooted social norms.

Research Fields: Applied Microeconomics · Household & Family Economics · Development Economics · Economics of Culture and Religion
