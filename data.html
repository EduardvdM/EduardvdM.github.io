Data from My Research

Preaching Against the Choir: Religious Leaders and Reproductive Norms in Kenya

Job Market Paper · 2026

  • Original survey of 635 women, 17 religious leaders, and 4 focus groups in Maua, Meru County, Kenya (2026)
  • Kenya Demographic and Health Survey: 2003, 2008, 2014, and 2022 waves
  • Confidential program records from CHAK and CCIH identifying the 21 health facilities that were involved in faith-based family planning advocacy
  • Kenya Master Health Facility Register: all registered health facilities, with facility type and church affiliation
  • Afrobarometer: Kenya, waves 2–8 (2003–2021)
  • World Values Survey: Kenya (2021)
  • IPUMS International: 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census
  • Spatial data: OpenStreetMap places of worship, AidData GeoQuery, and geoBoundaries administrative boundaries
